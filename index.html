About

I build things by understanding why they need to exist.

I'm Anik Pal, a frontend developer and UI designer who enjoys the full arc of building a product — sketching the interface, structuring the code behind it, and chasing down the small details that make an experience feel finished. I care about clean architecture as much as clean visuals, because the two are never really separate.

Most of what I know came from breaking things and figuring out why — debugging is where I actually learn a system, not just where I fix it. That habit of staying curious is what keeps me picking up new tools, languages, and ways of thinking about problems.
